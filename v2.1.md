# v2.1 — the UI pass

**Status: shipped.** `2.1.0`

v0.2 made the whole day fit one screen. v2.1 makes each of those fields *worth
looking at*: the right shape for the data it holds, a quieter surface behind it,
and an answer that always saves.

Three things happened:

1. **Every field answers.** Several could not be logged from the board at all.
2. **One field is gone.** Real connection tried to carry a category on its own.
3. **Every chart was redrawn.** Rings and dots became the shape each field's data
   actually has.

---

## 1. Fields that could not be logged

The board's quick-log sheet built the same widgets the guided flow uses, then
waited for them to call `onCommit`. Only the *preset* buttons ever did:

| Widget | Before | After |
| ------ | ------ | ----- |
| Slider (sleep, screen, outdoor, pickups) | dragging changed the number, never saved | releasing the slider saves |
| Counter (gym, alcohol, deep blocks) | `+`/`−` changed the number, never saved | saves 700 ms after the last tap |
| Time (rhythm: bed, wake) | picking a time did nothing | picking a time saves |
| Note (learned) | Enter saved; clicking away did not | Enter and clicking away both save |
| Toggle, scale | worked | unchanged |

The widgets now expose two callbacks with different meanings — `onCommit(answer)`
for a finished answer (a preset, a choice, `Continue`), `onLive(answer)` for a
value that settled while you were dragging. The guided `/log` flow uses only the
first plus a debounced live save, so its `Continue` button still drives the flow.
The board uses both: on the board, settling *is* answering.

A single-field tile still closes the sheet the moment it saves. Multi-field tiles
(rhythm, learned) stay open with a **Done** button, and **Clear** empties every
field the tile owns.

## 2. People is retired

"Real connection" was one counter carrying a whole category — and a ring, a
trend, an insight condition and a quadrant of the board all hung off it. One
field is not a dimension. It is gone: the field, the category, the ring.

The dashboard is now **three rings** — Body, Mind, Attention — and the tagline
follows: *One glance. Three rings. One score.*

Nothing else changed shape: the day score is still the mean of the categories
that have an answer, so no weight ever needed moving.

## 3. The catalogue: a shape per field

The chart is chosen by the shape of the data, not by taste. All of it lives in
`src/pulse/metrics.py` next to the fields themselves.

| Field | Shape | Why that shape |
| ----- | ----- | -------------- |
| Sleep | **range** | 7–9 h is a *band* to land in, not a target to beat. The track spans 0–12 h, the good band is shaded, the knob is where you slept. Past the band, the knob goes hollow. |
| Screen time | **range** | A ceiling you stay under. Same track, good zone 0–3 h. Over the line reads at a glance, with no red anywhere. |
| Pickups | **range** | Also a ceiling — 0–60 on a 0–150 track. |
| Steps | **bar** | A target you chase: 9,000 steps is the bar, capped at full. |
| Outdoor light | **bar** | Same idea: 20 minutes is the bar. |
| Stay clean | **bar** | 30 days clean, a slow bar that never resets to a daily zero. |
| Gym | **segments** (3) | A small count you tick off, one cell per session, weekly. |
| Alcohol-free | **segments** (7) | Seven days, one cell each. The week reads as the week. |
| Deep blocks | **blocks** (3) | The big count of the day *is* the shape: three slabs that fill. |
| Greens | **switch** | A yes/no that must look answered from across the room. |
| Creatine | **switch** | Same. |
| Read 10+ min | **switch** | Same. |
| Learned something | **switch** + note | The note rides in the caption of the same tile. |
| Mood | **scale** (5) | A felt step, drawn as five rising bars plus the word. |
| Energy | **scale** (5) | Same. |
| Rhythm | **night** | Bed and wake as a window across a 24-hour day, wrapping midnight. |
| Weight | **trend** | A number against its own history: 21 days of readings, with the goal as a dotted line. |

Chart vocabulary in code: `range`, `bar`, `segments`, `blocks`, `switch`,
`scale`, `night`, `trend`. The retired shapes (`gauge`, `clock`, `value`) are
gone, and so is every `.gauge-*` rule that drew them.

The two biggest range tiles — **sleep** and **screen time** — carry a second
view: the last seven readings drawn under the gauge, with the good zone still
shaded behind them, on its own scale. So the largest tile on the board answers
both questions at once — *where am I today*, and *where have I been* — instead of
being a small mark floating in a big rectangle. The gauge and the week share an
accent, so the tile reads as one object.

### What each tile says

- A tile carries its own raw values (`values`), its axis and good zone (`track`,
  `zone`), its goal (`goal`) and, for a trend or a gauge with history, the recent
  series (`series`) — the front end draws geometry without asking the server for
  anything else.
- Weekly tiles report the week, never a daily zero.
- Weight stays `optional`: it never counts toward "logged today".
- Units agree with their number: *1 day*, *2 days*, *1 session*, *2 sessions*.

## 4. The layout

Body has nine fields; Mind has five; Attention has three. So Body gets the full
height of the left column and the two smaller categories stack on the right:

```
┌────────┬────────────────┬────────────────┐
│  rail  │                │      MIND      │
│ rings  │      BODY      ├────────────────┤
│ score  │    (9 tiles)   │   ATTENTION    │
│ count  │                │    (3 tiles)   │
└────────┴────────────────┴────────────────┘
```

- Every band is a two-column grid, and all three pack exactly: 14 units into
  7 rows, 10 into 5, 6 into 3.
- Importance buys **rows**: `size 1/2/3` → one, two or three grid rows.
- Tablet (≤1024px): the rail becomes a header band, the three bands keep their
  columns. Phone (≤700px): the bands stack, and importance becomes *width* — a
  size-3 tile takes the whole row, everything else pairs up.
- The board is one screen at every size: no scrollbar, no clipped tile, from
  1728×1080 down to 360×640.

## 5. The surface

The palette was tightened to read like an instrument rather than a demo:

- Three accents, one per category, slightly desaturated: Body `#43d6a0`,
  Mind `#7d93f5`, Attention `#f2a94e`. An accent appears only where data was
  logged — never as decoration.
- Tiles are flat `#101215` with a hairline border. Empty tiles keep the dashed
  border; answered tiles lift by ~6% toward their accent and the marks take the
  accent colour.
- A yes/no answers loudly: the whole tile washes, the pill flips, and the value
  turns accent — three signals at once.
- Band headers are a label, an accent dot, a hairline rule and the count, the way
  a printed table sets a section.
- The rail counts the day and shows a three-stop meter (Body → Mind → Attention).
- Charts scale with the tile, not the window: `cqh`-based clamps, so a size-3
  gauge is chunky and a size-1 gauge is a hairline, both legible.
- Short tiles shed content in order — caption, then decorative chart, then the
  unit — and never the number.

## Tests

`uv run pytest` — **41 tests**, including new coverage for the range tiles
(axis and zone), the raw `values` a tile draws from, and the weight trend series.
`/tmp`-maintained Playwright harnesses (not in the repo) checked all 17 tiles at
five viewports: 0 console errors, no scroll, no clipped tile, and every field
answerable end to end.

## Known limits

- The weight trend needs a few readings before it says anything; with one, it
  draws a flat line and claims nothing.
- Editing still happens in the quick-log sheet. Answering *on* the board is
  [v2.2](v2.2.md).
- The night window is geometry only — a nap inside the window is not visible.
