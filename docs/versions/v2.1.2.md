# v2.1.2 — the home screen rework

**Status: shipped.** `2.1.2`

[v2.1.1](v2.1.1.md) made every field the right size. This pass makes the *whole
screen* one instrument: every card answers "how is today going, and what is left",
and the page is built so that reading it takes one glance rather than a scan.

The brief was a layout and visual pass — no new features. Two things crossed that
line on purpose, and both are recorded below: the cards became the only input on
Today (which pulled most of [v2.2](v2.2.md) forward), and the weight card lost its
trend sparkline, because history is not a Today thing.

---

## 1. The screen

Landscape first: at **1440×900** the whole board fits without scrolling, and the
same grid is used at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440, with cards and type scaling up and
the board capped at 1800px and centred.

**Left rail.** The three-ring score, with **yesterday** and **usual** as plain
numbers underneath. Then **Next best move**: the open field with the most points
left in it, its points in amber, one line of plain words, and a *Show card* button
that scrolls to the card and pulses it — it never logs. Then **This week**: seven
mini day rings, today boxed, and the week's average.

**Main area.** Three columns — Body, Mind, Attention — each with a dot in its hue,
a counter that matches the cards actually on screen (`6/9`), and its score. Each
column is a two-slot grid and **every row is full**: a card is one slot or two.

| Column | Rows |
| ------ | ---- |
| Body | Slept · Steps + Gym · Alcohol-free + Outdoor · Greens + Creatine · Weight · In bed |
| Mind | Deep blocks · Read + Learned · Mood · Energy |
| Attention | Screen time · Pickups · Clean day |

**Bottom bar.** *Where your score comes from*: one segmented bar, one segment per
scored field, **sized by weight**. Filled = earned, empty = still available,
dashed = not logged yet. Hovering shows `label · group · N% of the score · n/10`;
clicking scrolls to the card and pulses it. The bar is read-only, by design.

## 2. The card

Four parts, in this order, and nothing else:

1. **label and value** on the left, **points earned** on the right (`4/10`);
2. **the instrument**;
3. **one gap line** in plain words — *"1.2h past the band"*, *"2,200 to go"*,
   *"20 over your limit"*, *"5 of 7 days"*.

The gap line is the card's sentence: it says where today sits against where it
should land, so the number never has to be interpreted.

Narrow cards (a one-slot field at 1440px is only ~170px wide) put the label on its
own line and the number beneath it, so a long value can never eat the label. The
gap line is held to one line there, because a wrapped line would eat the
instrument's room.

## 3. Instruments, one per shape

| Shape | Fields | The instrument |
| ----- | ------ | -------------- |
| Range | Slept, Screen time, Pickups, Weight | A track with the target zone shaded, a marker for today (drag or tap to set), and a faint tick for yesterday |
| Bar | Steps, Outdoor light | A progress bar with a target tick and *N to go* |
| Weekly dashes | Gym, Alcohol-free | One dash per session / day of the week; tapping dash *i* sets the count to *i*; the last alcohol dash toggles today |
| Blocks | Deep blocks | Three large rectangles; each tap sets the count |
| 30 days | Clean day | Thirty dashes, oldest first, today at the end; tap toggles today |
| Yes / No | Greens, Creatine, Read, Learned | Two explicit segments, so *No* and *not logged* never look the same |
| Felt 1–5 | Mood, Energy | Five steps, with a small tick over your usual |
| Night | Slept's sibling, In bed | A day-long window with two draggable ends |

Weight lost its trend line: on Today it is a range around the goal (`goal ± 6`,
zone `goal ± 0.5`), because history belongs to Trends. It is optional, chip
*Optional*, and never contributes to the score.

The Learned card's **Yes** opens one text field below the segments — *"What did
you learn?"* — saved on Enter or 700ms after typing stops. It is the one keyboard
surface on the board.

## 4. States

- **Unlogged** — a dashed outline, a low-contrast value, an empty instrument.
- **Logged** — a solid border and a slightly lifted surface.
- **Target reached** — a small check mark next to the value, in the category hue.
- **Provisional** — while a category has unlogged fields the score reads `64` with
  `so far` under it, and that category's arc is drawn dashed.

Category hues appear in the rings, the dots, thin underlines and the check marks.
Amber is only ever "needs attention" or "next best move". No red anywhere.

## 5. The score behind the cards

The card payload grew so the cards could say what they say. Per tile:
`weight`, `score`, `points` (score × 10), `stake`, `gap`, `target`,
`target_label`, `yesterday`, `usual`, `step`, `min`, `labels`, `days` (the 30
dashes). The day also carries `board.next_move` (id, label, points, gap) and a
day-level `compare` (`yesterday`, `usual`, `usual_days`).

Weights sum to **3.0** — one per category. Mood, energy, creatine and weight are
`weight: null`: they are logged, they are shown, and they are chip-labelled *Not
in the score*, because a check-in is not a measurement. `stake` is
`weight × (1 − score) × 100/3`, so the next-best-move is simply the largest
stake left.

## 6. One screen, three layouts

The board is content-sized by default; only a wide **and** tall window stretches
it.

- **≥1181px and ≥861px tall** — the full stretch: rail + three columns fill the
  viewport, instruments grow into their rows (tracks to 104px, handles to 48px).
  This is the 1440×900 / 1920×1080 / 2560×1440 target.
- **≤1180px** — the rail folds into a band across the top, cards are content-sized
  (minimum 116px) and the page scrolls.
- **≤1000px** — one column of categories. **≤700px** — the rail stacks.
  **≤460px** — one card per row.
- **A 1440×900 window with a shorter viewport** (a laptop at 1440×800) falls back
  to the content-sized layout instead of squeezing six rows into 90px each.

The lesson worth writing down: `container-type: size` puts **size containment** on
the card, and size containment means the card's contents are ignored when the card
is sized. That is fine for a board that hands every card a fixed row, and fatal
for one that lets cards grow: the gap line was laid out but given ~0px, and every
height-based `cqh` size oscillated between layout passes. Cards now declare
`container: card / inline-size` — width queries still work, height is the content's
business — and the few sizes that must grow with the window are set once per
layout mode rather than per container height.

## 7. What was removed

- The quick-log sheet (`web/js/components/quickLog.js`) and its styles. On Today,
  the cards are the only way to log; the guided `/log` flow is untouched and is
  still the main mobile path.
- The old tile renderer (`web/js/components/tile.js`), with its trend sparkline
  and its eight chart shapes. `card.js` replaces it.
- `series` and `history` from the payload, and the "trend" chart kind.
- The `w/h` two-dimensional packing: the board is one slot per field or two, and
  the packing tests keep every row full.

## Tests

`uv run pytest` — **49 tests**.

Playwright, 15 viewports × two boards (the real one and the demo):

- no page scroll at 1440×900, 1920×1080, 2560×1440, 1200×900;
- no element inside any card is clipped or outgrows its row at any of the 15
  sizes, on either board;
- every page (`/`, `/log`, `/calendar`, `/trends`, `/insights`, `/demo`,
  `/settings`) at 1440×1080, 1920×1080 and 390×844: no console error, no
  horizontal overflow;
- driving the instruments in a real browser: blocks, gym dashes, Yes/No, the note
  field, dragging the sleep marker, arrow keys on a pickups slider, tapping a
  breakdown segment (logs nothing, pulses the card), *Show card* (logs nothing),
  and toggling today's clean day — each one lands in SQLite.

## Known limits

- Gap lines are short by design (`5 of 7 days`, not `5 of 7 days alcohol-free`).
  They must fit one line on a 130px-wide card.
- Clean day's number is a 30-day count while its score comes from the last 14
  days, so “18 of 30 days” can sit next to `0/10`. Both are honest; they measure
  different windows.
- The board is today-only. Past days open the day sheet from the week strip, and
  are edited in the calendar's day sheet, not on the board.
- Undo after an inline write is not built yet — that stays in
  [v2.2](v2.2.md).
