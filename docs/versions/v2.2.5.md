# v2.2.5: the board answers while you drag

**Status: shipped.** `2.2.5`

v2.2.4 gave the board its height back. Three things were still not right, and
all three were the same thing: the board answered after you finished, not
while you worked.

Scope: the board, on every screen. No scoring, no data, no new fields.

---

## 1. The sliders answer while you drag

- Dragging a rail on the board moved the knob, but the number only changed
  when the pointer came up. The number follows the pointer now: drag the
  Sleep knob and the hours move with it, drag the bed time and the clock
  moves with it.
- One write per drag, not one per pixel: the value is written once, on
  release. The editor's gauge already behaved this way — the board matches it
  now.
- The line of words under the card ("Inside 7–9 h") describes the value that
  was written. While the pointer is down it cannot describe a value that does
  not exist yet, so it shows the card's target ("Target 7–9 h"), and the
  server's sentence comes back on release.

## 2. Mood and energy become a ladder

- The card shows the editor's level meter: five bars of increasing height
  standing on a hairline, the word under each bar, the chosen one filled with
  the accent. A row of five equal chips says nothing about the scale; a
  ladder does.
- Clicking a bar answers. The card's value stays the word ("good").
- The usual value keeps a mark on the hairline, under the bar it belongs to.
- The ladder takes the height the card has, so the card stops being a small
  instrument in a large box. On the phone it is 88 px, on the desktop it
  fills the card.

## 3. The Attention column gets its answers

- The Main task card was the one that looked empty: two 44 px buttons at the
  top of a 151 px card, and fifty pixels of nothing under them. The answer
  fills the space the card has — the buttons grow to the height of the stage,
  the way the editor's doors do. The pairs (Read/Learned, Greens/Creatine)
  have a 44 px stage and keep their 44 px buttons, and Read and Learned stay
  identical.
- The limit rails (Screen time, Pickups) keep the big stage they now have.

## 4. Two things found on the way

- The card's `aria-valuetext` did not move with the drag, so a screen reader
  heard the old value while the pointer was down. It follows the value now.
- The knob on the thin rails (Steps, Outdoor light, Weight) was 15 px in a
  14 px rail: it read as a dot, not a handle. It grows with the rail.

## Verification

- **59 tests** (`uv run pytest -q`) — the payload, the scoring and the board
  are untouched.
- **Playwright at 1440×900**: dragging the Sleep, Screen time and In bed
  rails moves the number with the pointer, the gap line shows the target
  while the pointer is down and the server's sentence after it, and the
  `aria-valuetext` follows. One write lands on release.
- **The ladder**: clicking a bar answers, the card's value follows, and the
  usual mark stands on the hairline.
- **The board still fills**: a full day, an empty day and a day with both
  notes open — no page scroll, the bar at the bottom, every column 13 px
  above it, no two cards overlapping.
- **Every page** at 1440×900: no horizontal overflow, no console errors.
