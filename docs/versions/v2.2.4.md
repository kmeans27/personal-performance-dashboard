# v2.2.4 — the column fills

**Status: shipped.** `2.2.4`

v2.2.2 had the right physical scale: the board felt like an instrument panel.
v2.2.3 took it away, to stop one card stretching to match another, and left
Mind 216 px and Attention 235 px above the score bar. This pass gives the
height back without the stretching. The board is now three columns of a
different kind: a column is a stack of rows, a row is a pair of cards, and
every category has its own plan for how tall its rows are. The column fills
the height it has; the height a card cannot use goes into the field it draws.

Scope: desktop only. No scoring, no data, no copy. Tablet and phone keep the
content-sized layout until v2.3.

---

## 1. The column, not the row

- The board is three category columns. Each column is a stack of rows, and a
  row is a pair of cards: Steps beside Gym, Read beside Learned. A field that
  takes the whole width is a row of its own.
- A column fills the height between its header and the score bar. At 1440×900
  the last card of every column ends 13 px above the bar, the bar sits at the
  bottom of the screen, and the page does not scroll.
- **The columns do not share row heights.** Body's six rows, Mind's four and
  Attention's four are three independent plans. A row in Mind never grows to
  line up with a row in Body, and no card is stretched to match a card
  somewhere else.
- A card that cannot use the height — Read, Learned, Main task — keeps its
  44 px buttons at the top and leaves the rest of the room in the stage under
  them. The buttons are never stretched to fill a card.
- Nothing is invented to fill space: no filler card, no help text, no history
  chart. A column that genuinely has nothing to show ends short.

## 2. The row plan

Every category carries its own weights, and a row keeps its own minimum
height — a box that needs 103 px keeps 103 px even when its weight asks for
less. The leftover is spread by weight, never by stretching.

| Category | Rows and their weights |
| -------- | ---------------------- |
| **Body** | Slept 1.2 · Steps/Gym 1 · Alcohol/Outdoor 1 · Greens/Creatine 1.1 · Weight 1 · In bed 1.2 |
| **Mind** | Deep blocks 1.35 · Read/Learned 0.95 · Mood 1.4 · Energy 1.4 |
| **Attention** | Screen time 1.25 · Pickups 1.25 · Main task 1.1 · Clean day 1.35 |

Mind and Attention are no longer short. Body fills by modest growth: its six
cards are close to their natural heights already, and they are left there —
the plan proposed 118–128 px for Slept and 118–124 px for Greens, and the day
allows 116 and 125, because Body has almost no slack to give away.

**The rows are flex items, not grid tracks.** A grid track that cannot shrink
below its content pushes its neighbour out of the way; a flex row squeezes
its neighbours instead. That is the whole difference between the note row
overlapping the card below it and the note row taking the height from the
other rows in its own column.

## 3. The field scale

The height a card has left goes into the field, never into the space around
it. The head stays at the top of the card, the field takes what is left, and
the line of words stays at the bottom.

- **Tracks** thicken where the card has the room for it. The hairline under
  Steps, Outdoor light and Weight stays 14 px, because those cards are small;
  under Slept it is 26 px, and the knob stays a target you can hit.
- **The limit rails** (Screen time, Pickups) fill their card: an 81 px rail,
  the band that says "under 3 h" at 89 px, and a 54 px knob — as it was in
  v2.2.2. The plan asked for 28–36 px; on a 171 px card that would have left
  the rail marooned in the middle of an empty field, so the rail grew instead.
- **Deep blocks** are 97 px tall, three across, and the next block's dashed
  outline and `+` grow with them.
- **Mood and energy** keep one row of five chips, now 60 px tall, with the
  prompt above them. Both stay unscored.
- **Clean day** fills its card: thirty cells in 10 × 3, each about 31 px.
- **Yes/No** keeps the 44 px cap and stays at the top of the card.
- **Read, Learned, Main task** grow to the height of their row and no further.
- The night strip takes the height it has (17 px), the weight scale keeps its
  hairline, and the copy never grows to fill a card.

## 4. The note belongs to the pair

- A note row belongs to the pair it annotates. It opens below the pair, full
  width, outside the card, 38 px including the input.
- Opening it grows that pair's row and takes the height from the other rows
  in the same column. It does not shift every card below it, and it never
  pushes the score bar off the screen.
- With both notes open at 1440×900 the cards give up a few pixels each and
  stay above their natural minimum. The bar does not move.

## 5. The breakpoints

- The fill runs at `min-width: 1240px` and `min-height: 820px`, and it is
  active at 1440×900.
- Below either threshold — 1180×900, the 700 and the 390 layouts — the board
  goes back to sizing every card to its own content and the page scrolls. The
  fill is never forced into a short or narrow window.
- The growth is capped: the board is 809 px tall at 1440×900 and is not
  allowed past 972, so a tall window leaves the extra room below the board
  instead of turning every card into a billboard. At 1500×1000 the cards are
  already 1.2× their 900 height, which is as much as they should grow.
- The rail is unchanged: it stacks from the top, with nothing pinned to the
  bottom.

## 6. What stays

- The v2.2.3 rail: the larger ring, Yesterday and Usual, the three next best
  moves, the week, the Sources card.
- Main task and the scoring, notes as rows, the grouped score bar, the
  perfect-day moment, the contrast ramp, the violet Mind.
- Content-sized tablet and phone layouts.
- No new fields, settings, data, gradients, confetti, emoji or red.
- No "SHOW CARD" button, no pinned rail bottom, no equalisation across
  columns.

## Verification

- **59 tests** (`uv run pytest -q`) — the payload, the scoring and the board
  are untouched.
- **Playwright at 1440×900**, a full day, an empty day and a day with both
  notes open: the page does not scroll, the bar sits at the bottom, every
  column ends 13 px above it, no two cards overlap, and the note row opens
  without moving the bar.
- **The same board at 1180×900, 1240×820, 1500×1000, 1920×1080 and
  390×844**: content-sized and scrolling below the threshold, filled above
  it, and no horizontal overflow anywhere.
- **Clicking through**: answering Yes under Learned and Main task opens the
  note rows, typing in one saves it, and the other column answers do not
  disturb the layout.

## Known limits

- **Body is at its natural size.** Six cards in one column leave almost no
  slack: Slept lands 2 px under the band the plan asked for, and Greens 1 px
  over it. Widening the gap between rows would fix both and cost the columns
  their air, so it was left alone.
- **The limit rails are taller than the plan asked for.** 28–36 px was the
  proposal; the shipped rail fills the card instead, because the alternative
  is a small rail in a large empty field.
- **The demo board is a picture** — it keeps the content-sized layout,
  because it is a scrolling page and not the home screen.
