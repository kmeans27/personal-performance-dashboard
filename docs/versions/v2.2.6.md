# v2.2.6: the ladder you can see, the number you can watch

**Status: shipped.** `2.2.6`

v2.2.5 put the editor's ladder on Mood and Energy, and made the card's number
follow a drag. On the board the result fell short: the unselected bars were so
close to the card's own surface that the cards read as empty, and the live
number was small, dim and only reachable on the thin rail itself. v2.2.6
finishes the two pieces without changing the board's layout or the score.

Scope: the board, on every screen. No scoring, no data, no new fields.

---

## 1. The ladder is a chart you can read

- Mood and Energy now draw the editor's ladder inside a recessed stage, the
  same neutral panel the detail sheet uses: a darker field, a hairline to
  stand the bars on, five ascending rectangles, and the word under each.
- The unselected bars are visibly filled, not a ghost against the card. The
  chosen bar is the category accent; the usual value keeps its mark on the
  hairline. Rough → great and empty → full read as scales again, not as five
  labels floating in an empty card.
- The height still comes from the card. The spacer and the bar share the
  space above the words in the same 0.17 / 0.38 / 0.58 / 0.79 / 1 ratio the
  editor uses, so no browser has to multiply a percentage by a number inside
  `calc()` to draw the steps.
- Clicking a bar answers, as before. The card's value stays the word.

## 2. The whole field answers the hand

- The rail was a 14 px strip inside a much taller instrument; a drag that
  started a few pixels above or below it did nothing. The whole instrument is
  the drag target now — the field around the rail, the knob, the track — for
  the gauges and the night strip alike.
- Pointer moves are followed on the window, not only while the pointer is
  over the rail, so a drag that leaves the card keeps moving the value. A
  pointer cancel, a lost capture or the window losing focus finishes the
  drag exactly once.
- The number and its unit follow the pointer: `9.5 h`, `7.75`, `00:30`. The
  card brightens and its border takes the category accent while it is held,
  so the preview cannot be mistaken for the old value. The line of words
  shows the target until the write lands, then the server's own sentence.
- One write per drag, on release. A server update that arrives while the
  pointer is still down no longer pulls the number or the knob away from the
  value under the hand. Keyboard arrows, Home and End still work and still
  write immediately.

## 3. Nothing else moved

- The one-screen 1440×900 board is unchanged: same rows, same weights, same
  score bar, same Sources card, same perfect-day moment. The ladder's stage
  fits inside the card it already had.
- A note opening under a pair still takes its height from the other rows in
  the column.
- Mobile remains the content-sized column; the ladder reads there too.

## Verification

- **59 tests** (`uv run pytest -q`) — payload, scoring and board data are
  untouched.
- **Playwright at 1440×900**: dragging Sleep, Screen time, Pickups, Weight,
  Outdoor light and In bed updates the number and unit while the pointer is
  down and keeps one write for release. Dragging from the top of the Sleep
  field, not the thin rail, works. Keyboard arrows update the number without
  leaving the card in a held state.
- **The ladder at 1440×900 and 390×844**: five ascending bars, visible
  unselected fill, the selected bar in the accent, words under the bars, no
  overflow, no console errors. The empty state reads as a ladder rather than
  an empty card.
- **The board still fills**: empty day, full day and both notes open — no page
  scroll at 1440×900, the bar at the bottom.
